The Driving Force Behind our Firm

Yolanda Álvarez

Founding Attorney · Álvarez Legal LLC

Yolanda Álvarez is a dedicated, innovative, and passionate attorney. Her groundbreaking approach and willingness to challenge established norms have shaped our working philosophy.

With a successful career in intellectual property, civil and commercial litigation, and entertainment law, she has demonstrated her ability to represent clients in intricate legal matters at both the local and federal levels. Also, she is a prominent advocate for animal rights. As president of FePA (Federación Protectora de Animales de Puerto Rico), she is a tireless proponent of laws and policies that safeguard animals and promote their well-being.

At Álvarez Legal, we embrace Yolanda's disruptive and courageous approach. We take pride in offering our clients exceptional service, prompt responses, and innovative solutions that embody our commitment to legal excellence.
